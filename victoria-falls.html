Discover Victoria Falls

Victoria Falls (Mosi-oa-Tunya) is Zambia’s premier destination for luxury travel, adventure, and wildlife.

Why Visit

Victoria Falls combines iconic scenery with world-class activities: helicopter tours, Devil’s Pool, white-water rafting and luxury riverside lodges.

Perfect for honeymooners, photographers, and adventure travelers.

Visitors can combine adrenaline activities with relaxing sunset cruises, guided wildlife viewing nearby, and exclusive lodge experiences overlooking the Zambezi.

  • Devil’s Pool swimming (seasonal)
  • Helicopter & microlight flights
  • White-water rafting and bungee jumping
  • Luxury riverside lodges & spas
  • Wildlife viewing in Mosi-oa-Tunya National Park
