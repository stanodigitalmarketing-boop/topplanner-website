Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common membership questions

How do I become a member? +
Simply fill out the registration form above and select your preferred membership tier. You'll receive immediate access to member benefits upon completion.
Can I upgrade my membership? +
Yes, you can upgrade your membership at any time. The price difference will be prorated, and you'll immediately gain access to the higher tier benefits.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept all major credit cards, PayPal, bank transfers, and mobile money payments. Annual memberships can also be paid monthly with a small fee.
Is there a cancellation policy? +
Memberships can be cancelled at any time. You'll retain access to benefits until the end of your billing period. No cancellation fees apply.
Do benefits apply to group travel? +
Yes, membership benefits apply to all bookings made through our agency, including group travel arrangements and family packages.
